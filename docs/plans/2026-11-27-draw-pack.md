## What is sold

`bound-draws-100`: 1 USDC on Base mainnet buys a pack of 100 draws (a cent a draw), through the x402
route that already settles. The receipt's `download_url` carries the pack token. Each
`GET /x402/draw?token=<token>&bind=<hex>` returns 32 bytes fixed by the caller's bind, the public beacon
pulse current when it is served, and the Worker's Ed25519 receipt key: the SHA-256 of the signature over
the record. Neither side chooses it and anyone can check it offline.

What it is not: a secret (a draw must not be used as a key); independent (Ledatic runs the beacon and
holds the key); exactly counted (KV is eventually consistent, so two draws in the same instant can share
an index). The public pulse stays free.

- Hours: out of Fieldcraft's, by the owner's decision tonight.
