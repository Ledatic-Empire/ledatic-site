Provisioning & galley

Fresh stores, dry goods, bonded items arranged with your chandler before arrival. We watch consumption per vessel and push the order at the right ETA window — not too early to spoil, not too late to scramble.

⌬

Water & pump-out

Holding-tank levels, potable-water tanks and grey-water schedules drive a single coordinated call to the port's waste contractor and water boat. Crossing a regulatory threshold mid-voyage triggers an alert, not a violation.

⛽

Bunkering & stores

Fuel state, lube oil, spare parts and crew rotations roll into a single arrival packet for your agent. Bunker barge confirmed, deliveries gated to the dock, paperwork pre-filed with the harbor master.
