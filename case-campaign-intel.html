What we're doing

We're running a 30-day campaign-intelligence POC for a regional client. They wanted a regular drumbeat of intel on a market they compete in, without burning an analyst's full week on manual scraping, and without sending their clients' material through a third-party AI vendor. We thought we could put a small pipeline in front of those three constraints. The POC is how we find out.

The brief shape

We landed on a Mon / Wed / Fri rhythm. Each day targets a different decision surface, so the week reads as a sequence rather than three copies of the same digest:

Monday — Arena Map / what's in the market this week / notable moments / a suggested play for the week Wednesday — Per-angle Deep-dive / longevity-winners by angle / variation winners / creative brief, ready to spec Friday — Verdict / HIT / PARTIAL / MISS header / cross-week arc / market snapshot + movement

Friday checks itself against Monday. We don't claim that loop is perfect — we claim it's the smallest honest feedback shape we could build, and that having one matters more than not having one.

How it actually runs

The pipeline lives on hardware we own. There are no public endpoints reachable from the open internet; everything is on a private fabric. Briefs are delivered to a private channel. The client's source material never crosses our wire to a third-party model.

Every brief is bound to a pulse_id from our live plasma physics beacon at the moment of delivery, signed by an Ed25519 key held on a Pi at our edge. Months from now the client can re-derive the digest, fetch the witness pubkey from /attest/fleet0.pub.pem, and verify the signature with the same one-liner we publish on every Rail release. That's the same primitive Ledatic uses for itself, not a separate marketing layer. See /entropy and /system.

What we're learning

We're still mid-engagement, so we'll be careful with claims. What we'll say honestly: the discipline of shipping a structured brief three times a week sharpens the questions we ask of the data more than we expected, and the attestation step has been less of a tax than we thought it would be once the cron was wired. We'll write more about both after the POC closes.

If this fits your shop

We're early. We're not pretending to be a powerhouse. This page is about one engagement, but most of what we do is smaller — a weekend script, a one-day tool, a tiny watcher for one thing you care about. If anything here resembles a thing you wish were easier, tell us about it and we'll figure out the smallest version that would actually help. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.
