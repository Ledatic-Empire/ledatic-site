FAQs
Can I use this for [my use case]?
Probably. The POC proved the model on legal case litigation and institutional abuse ad monitoring. But the core system is vertically agnostic — competitive intelligence, regulatory monitoring, market research, fraud detection. If you have daily data to monitor and patterns to surface, it works.
Where does my data live?
By default, data is stored and processed on hardware we own and operate in Detroit. All LLM inference is local — your data never touches a cloud API. On-premise deployment is available for engagements that need it.
What's "Provenance" and why would I want it?
For regulated workflows (legal discovery, EU AI Act compliance, financial audit), every report is cryptographically signed by a dedicated physical witness device — separate from the machine that generated it — and chained to a public entropy beacon. Auditors verify the chain in their own browser — no backend trust required. See the chain or verify a real signed report.
Can I ask custom questions against the corpus?
Yes. Engagements can include access to a token-gated Q&A portal. Ask the 30-day (or longer) corpus anything. Answered by the local LLM in seconds.
What if your LLM goes down?
Briefs are queued and regenerated, and you’re notified of any delay. Engagements that need stronger guarantees — including self-hosting — are scoped individually.
Do you have a contract?
Engagements are scoped individually — month-to-month is typical, longer terms negotiable. We don't lock you in on data; all your briefs, digests, and the corpus are exportable in standard formats.
